# Framio user research plan

**Goal:** decide which features to build next, define core personas, and sharpen positioning — in 2–3 weeks, pre-launch, using free channels (TikTok/IG, Discord/Reddit, friends & classmates, Facebook groups).

**Core hypothesis to test:** *people take photobooth strips primarily to post them — Reels, TikTok stories — not to keep them.* If this is true, sharing/export features beat capture features in priority. If false (strips are kept as private mementos), the roadmap looks very different.

---

## 1. Research questions

| Decision | Research questions |
|---|---|
| Personas | Who takes booth-style photos online? Alone, with a partner, with friends? On phone or laptop? How often, and on what occasions? |
| Features | What do they do with the result? Which matters more: more looks (filters/frames), more formats (strips/grids), remote co-shooting, or easier posting? |
| Positioning | What words do they use for this activity? What alternatives do they use today (Photobooth app, TikTok filters, Korean booth apps like life4cut)? What would make Framio "theirs"? |

## 2. Method mix (fits 2–3 weeks, $0 budget)

**A. Screener + survey** — quantitative base. Target 80–150 responses. 3–4 minutes, mobile-first (most respondents will open it from TikTok/IG/Discord). Tools: Google Forms or Tally (free).

**B. 5–6 demo interviews** — qualitative depth. 25–30 min video calls with the most interesting survey respondents. Since Framio is pre-launch, the demo *is* the stimulus: watch them take a strip live (camera reveal, sounds, themes), then talk. Friends-of-friends are fine at this stage; avoid interviewing only close friends (they'll be too nice).

**C. Social listening (passive, ongoing)** — 30 min/day scrolling: TikTok tags (#photobooth, #photostrip, #life4cut, #photoboothchallenge), Reddit (r/VintageDigitalCameras, r/Polaroid adjacent), Discord aesthetic servers. Screenshot anything that shows how people share strips. This directly pressure-tests your Reels/story assumption with zero recruiting.

## 3. Proto-personas to validate

Draft these as hypotheses now; the survey either confirms, splits, or kills them.

**The Bestie Documentarian** — 16–24, takes strips with friends at sleepovers/hangouts, posts to stories same-day. Cares about: looks cute, fast to post, shows the friendship. Success metric for Framio: strips shared per session.

**The Long-Distance Duo** — couple or best friends apart; wants to take a strip *together remotely*. Cares about: synchronized capture, the ritual. This persona justifies a big feature (remote co-booth) — only build it if they show up in the data.

**The Aesthetic Curator** — 18–28, feed has a consistent look; uses ASCII/Game Boy/retro modes as content material. Cares about: unique effects nobody else has, export quality, no watermarks.

**The Nostalgia Hobbyist** — any age, loves instant cameras and retro tech; the camera reveal animation is the product for them. Cares about: authenticity of the ritual, print-like output. Possibly a smaller but loud, loyal group.

## 4. Survey draft

**Screener (Q1–Q2)**
1. How old are you? (Under 16 / 16–19 / 20–24 / 25–29 / 30+) — *under 16 → thank & end*
2. In the last 3 months, have you taken fun/aesthetic photos of yourself or friends (booth apps, filters, instant cameras, photo strips)? (Yes / No) — *No → thank & end*

**Behavior (Q3–Q7)**
3. Who do you usually take these photos with? (Alone / Partner / Friends in person / Friends remotely / Family) *(multi)*
4. What device? (Phone / Laptop–webcam / Real photobooth / Instant camera) *(multi)*
5. What do you do with the results? (Post to story–Reel–TikTok / Send in group chat–DM / Keep in camera roll / Print / Set as wallpaper–lockscreen) *(multi — this is the hypothesis question)*
6. How often? (Weekly+ / Monthly / Few times a year / Only special occasions)
7. What apps/tools do you currently use for this? *(open text — positioning gold)*

**Feature appeal (Q8–Q9)**
8. Rank these 1–5: More filter looks · Photo strips with cute frames · Retro effects (ASCII, Game Boy) · Take a booth remotely with a friend · One-tap share to story
9. "A photo strip prints out of a little instant camera on your screen, with sound" — how does that land? (Love it, I'd screen-record it / Cute but wouldn't share / Not for me)

**Positioning (Q10–Q11)**
10. What would you call an app like this to a friend? *(open text — steal their words)*
11. Complete: "I'd actually use this if ___" *(open text)*

**Recruit hook (Q12)**
12. Want to try it early and chat for 20 min? Drop your @/email. *(interview pipeline)*

## 5. Interview guide (30 min)

1. **Warm-up (3 min)** — how they spend time online, last photo they posted.
2. **Context (7 min)** — walk me through the last time you took fun photos with someone; what happened to those photos after?
3. **Live demo (10 min)** — they open Framio, take a strip. *Say nothing during the reveal.* Watch: do they smile, screen-record, ask how to save? Then: "what would you do with this right now?"
4. **Reaction (7 min)** — feature card sort: show 5 feature cards (more themes, remote booth, story-sized export, custom frame editor, GIF/motion strips), have them pick two and say why.
5. **Wrap (3 min)** — "what would make you open this again next week?" + who else should I talk to (snowball recruiting).

## 6. Distribution plan

| Channel | Play | Expected yield |
|---|---|---|
| TikTok/IG | Post the camera-reveal screen recording (it's inherently shareable) + link-in-bio to survey | 30–60 responses if one post lands |
| Discord/Reddit | Aesthetic/retro-tech servers & subs; ask mods first; lead with the GB-mode screenshot | 20–40 quality responses |
| Friends & classmates | Direct sends; ask each to forward to 2 people (dilutes friend bias) | 20–30 fast responses |
| Facebook groups | Photography/photobooth/K-culture groups | 10–20, likely older skew — useful contrast |

Incentive: early-access "founder" badge or first custom frame named after them — free and on-brand.

## 7. Timeline (2.5 weeks)

| Days | Work |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Finalize survey, set up form, prep demo build & social clips |
| 3–9 | Field the survey across all channels; social listening daily; book interviews from Q12 |
| 8–13 | Run 5–6 interviews (overlaps fielding) |
| 14–16 | Synthesis: affinity-map interview notes, cut survey by persona candidates, feature impact/effort matrix |
| 17 | Decide: top 2 personas, top 3 features, one-line positioning |

## 8. Synthesis & decision rules (pre-committed)

- A proto-persona is **validated** if ≥20% of qualified respondents match its behavior pattern *and* at least one interviewee brings it to life.
- **Remote co-booth** gets built only if "friends remotely" (Q3) ≥25% *or* two interviewees ask for it unprompted.
- The Reels/story hypothesis is **confirmed** if "post to story/Reel/TikTok" (Q5) is the #1 answer — then one-tap story-sized export becomes the next feature regardless of ranking answers (watch what they do, not what they rank).
- Positioning line comes from Q10 verbatims: pick the phrase that appears ≥3 times.

## 9. Risks

Friend-heavy sample inflates positivity — weight strangers' responses higher. Survey rankings overstate "cool" features vs. habitual ones — trust Q5 (actual behavior) over Q8 (stated preference). Pre-launch demo effect: the reveal wow factor may mask everyday retention questions — hence the "open it again next week" interview question.
